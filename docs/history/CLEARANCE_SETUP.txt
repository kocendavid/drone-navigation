What the node measures
----------------------
Chassis FLU axes: +X forward, +Y left, +Z up. The first corridor is
X=0.2..15 m, Y=-0.5..0.5 m, Z=-0.3..0.5 m relative to chassis.
Range filtering occurs in sensor coordinates at 1..30 m. Sensor near range
is 1 m; missing returns inside that blind region never establish clearance.
Transforms into chassis and world use each cloud's acquisition timestamp.
Missing transforms get at most 0.3 wall seconds to arrive.

Five valid stationary scans calibrate a broad horizontal floor in the lower
half of world-coordinate returns. The fit requires at least 15% point support,
at least 1 m span along both horizontal axes, slope below 0.5 degrees,
95th-percentile residual below 1.5 cm, and floor heights stable within 2 cm.
Returns less than 12 cm above that estimated floor are excluded. This is a
flat test-room wall/box experiment; low objects in the excluded strip and
unobserved geometry are not represented by this baseline.
Use --floor-z VALUE only for an independently measured world floor height.
Do not assume the room floor lies at world Z=0.

CLEAR = closest observed forward surface is farther than 2 m.
HOLD = closest observed forward surface is at or inside 2 m.
UNKNOWN = no forward returns, malformed input, wrong frame, repeated/zero/
backwards stamps, missing TF, clock disagreement, no advancing clock or stale
cloud. Wall-time watchdog becomes UNKNOWN after 1 s even if /clock is paused.
CLEAR describes observed surfaces; it does not certify a free flight volume.
Voxelization retains the closest original sample per cell and the decision
uses minimum X before voxelization. Downsampling cannot erase a HOLD point.
