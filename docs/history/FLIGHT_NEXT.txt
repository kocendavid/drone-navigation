It selects Fast DDS/domain 0, subscribes for eight wall-clock seconds, then

It reports heading quality, position validity, arming/mode/failsafe, landed

and position check, not permission to fly or proof of the correct simulator.

------------------------------------------------

   commander takeoff, then wait for the native 1 m hover to settle.
2. In a separate ROS Jazzy/Pixi terminal, run:
   C:\Users\finel\Documents\ChatGPT\issaac\tools\ros2_hover.cmd --height 1.0
   It captures current local X/Y and heading, and targets local NED Z=-1 m.
   Wait for READY after five seconds of fresh targets with PX4 readers.
3. At the PX4 pxh> prompt, enter commander mode offboard.
   Confirm Offboard is accepted; observe a stationary hover for 10 seconds.
   If it is rejected, report the console reason rather than forcing the mode.
4. At pxh> enter commander land. After landing mode is accepted, stop the
   ROS publisher with Ctrl+C. Observe landing and disarming.
   To stop the test while airborne, select landing first, not Ctrl+C.

How the drone will discover its room
----------------------------------
Proposed first exploration behavior (not yet implemented): fixed altitude,
small horizontal steps, low speed, stop and scan between steps.
Build a map with occupied, observed free and unknown cells. Empty point-cloud
regions alone are not free-space evidence; use validated sensor rays and
clearance for the aircraft volume. For the first map, Isaac world TF is
simulation ground-truth localization. Later localization can use onboard
estimates; the map/world and PX4 local-origin alignment must be measured.
Select a reachable viewpoint at the boundary of observed free and unknown
space, plan through observed free space, follow a bounded trajectory, stop,
update the map and repeat. This is frontier exploration. A simple geometric
policy is an understandable baseline; it needs no trained model.
PX4 tracks the trajectory and controls motors. The exploration policy picks
where to go. Later a learned policy could rank viewpoints; evaluate it on
new rooms against this baseline, measuring coverage, collisions, clearance,
time and inference latency. Learning is a later experiment, not a claim
that the current drone already knows how to explore.
